# Theory Memo 006 — PAM as an Empirical Instrument for RIG

**Status:** Canonical bridge memo  
**Scope:** PAM observatory lineage through OBS-082

## Purpose

This memo defines the relationship between PAM and RIG.

PAM is the empirical instrument through which RIG hypotheses are measured, registered, and audited.

PAM is not proof of a universal RIG theory.

## Instrument model

A compact representation is:

\[
\Omega
\rightarrow
X
\rightarrow
C(X)
\rightarrow
T(C(X))
\rightarrow
S(R,C,K,T\mid\Omega)
\rightarrow
r
\rightarrow
\operatorname{Audit}(r).
\]

In words:

1. declare the artifact context;
2. identify artifact objects;
3. measure them through carriers;
4. apply transformations under contracts;
5. compare relation survival;
6. register the result;
7. audit diagnostic maturity.

## What PAM measures

### Scale-space structure

OBS-076 exposed multiscale persistence, migration, coalescence, and factorization across C/Cp2/Cp3.

### Transition geometry

OBS-077 connected scale-space transition structure to path-label cohorts and window-local divergence and boundedness.

### Local stability features

OBS-078 identified a compact three-feature stability core:

- `mean_lambda_local_mean`;
- `mean_delta_d_mean`;
- `bounded_share_mean`.

### Robustness

OBS-079 tested the compact core through leave-structure-out validation, bootstrap confidence intervals, and pairwise stability-axis decomposition.

### Contract stability

OBS-080 tested whether the relation survived numeric transformations, scale-band restrictions, feature-family projections, and structural resampling.

### Registry

OBS-081 converted contract-sensitivity evidence into relation × carrier records.

### Readiness audit

OBS-082 tested whether those records were sufficiently contrasted, localized, and repair-specific to support intervention hypotheses.

## What PAM currently supports

Within the current artifact lineage, PAM supports:

- structured regime differences;
- transition-, cohort-, and window-relative organization;
- a compact diagnostic carrier;
- robustness under tested structural validations;
- contract-stable reusable structure;
- a relation × carrier registry;
- a readiness audit;
- a local empirical RIG apparatus.

## What PAM does not currently support

PAM does not currently establish:

- intervention readiness;
- causal control;
- successful repair;
- direct failure-site localization;
- universal invariance;
- model-independent learning theory;
- external generalization;
- formal topology.

## Why the distinction matters

PAM can show that a relation is visible, robust, and reusable within the current lineage.

That does not mean RIG has become a universal theory.

The empirical instrument can mature before the theory generalizes.

The correct claim is:

> PAM operationalizes a local empirical program for studying reusable invariance.

The incorrect claim is:

> PAM proves a general theory of cognition or learning.

## Implications for the next phase

The next stage should strengthen:

- negative-control contrast;
- direct failure localization;
- repair specificity.

It should not jump directly to intervention.
