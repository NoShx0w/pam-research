# Theory Memo 008 — Claim Ladder and Scope Discipline for PAM/RIG

**Status:** Canonical claim-governance memo  
**Scope:** PAM/RIG artifact lineage through OBS-083

## Purpose

This memo defines the levels of claim that PAM/RIG evidence may support.

Its purpose is to prevent evidence at one maturity level from being narrated as if it belonged to a stronger level.

## Level 0 — Observed artifact pattern

A measured or computed structure appears in the current artifacts.

Examples include:

- Fisher/MDS organization;
- signed phase structure;
- Lazarus or response fields;
- scale-space support.

Allowed wording:

- “we observe”;
- “the artifact contains”;
- “the measurement shows.”

Not allowed:

- “this proves a mechanism”;
- “this is universal”;
- “this is causal”;
- “this is formal topology.”

## Level 1 — Robust diagnostic pattern

The observed pattern persists across declared checks, resamples, or holdouts.

OBS-079 is a primary example.

Allowed wording:

- “robust within the tested artifact lineage.”

Not allowed:

- reusable under untested contracts;
- actionable;
- causal;
- universal.

## Level 2 — Reusable invariant

A relation survives declared transformations, contracts, or carrier comparisons.

OBS-080 is the primary example.

Allowed wording:

- “reusable within the tested contracts.”

Not allowed:

- universal invariance;
- intervention readiness;
- external generalization.

## Level 3 — Registered invariant

The reusable relation is represented as a relation × carrier evidence record.

OBS-081 is the primary example.

Registration improves inspectability and comparison.

It does not imply readiness.

## Level 4 — Diagnostic invariant

The registered invariant supports stable structural interpretation but remains limited in negative-control contrast, failure localization, or repair specificity.

OBS-082 and OBS-083 establish this as the strongest current RIG level.

## Level 5 — Candidate-ready invariant

A future category requiring:

- strong negative-control contrast;
- direct failure localization;
- specific repair evidence;
- no fatal provenance or leakage blockers.

Current artifacts do not establish this level.

## Level 6 — Repair-hypothesis-ready invariant

A candidate-ready record attached to a falsifiable repair hypothesis:

\[
H_\rho=(R,C,Z,\rho,M,R^{-},\Phi,\Omega).
\]

No current examples exist.

## Level 7 — Actionable invariant

A future maturity category in which a repair hypothesis is sufficiently grounded for a carefully controlled intervention test.

Current artifacts do not establish this level.

## Level 8 — Causal or control claim

Requires intervention, causal identification, or another appropriate causal design.

PAM/RIG currently does not support this level.

## Level 9 — Universal theory claim

Would require evidence across models, corpora, provenance lineages, external contexts, and a substantially stronger formal theory.

PAM/RIG currently does not support this level.

## Cross-level examples

### Compact stability core

OBS-078–080 support Levels 1–2.

OBS-081–083 support Levels 3–4.

They do not support Levels 5–9.

### Seam

The seam is an observed and robust structural substrate.

It is not automatically a failure surface, causal mechanism, or formal topological boundary.

### Obstruction

Obstruction is an operational diagnostic of path dependence or transport mismatch.

It is not automatically a direct failure site.

### Proto-groupoid

Proto-groupoid language is a historical operational analogy.

It is not a formal groupoid claim.

## Supported current claims

Within the current artifact lineage:

- PAM observes structured regime patterns;
- the three-feature stability core is robust under tested validations;
- the core survives declared contracts;
- reusable evidence can be represented in a registry;
- readiness can be audited;
- current records are diagnostic-only;
- OBS-083 separates contrast-limited from localization-limited records.

## Unsupported current claims

The current evidence does not support:

- candidate-readiness;
- repair-hypothesis readiness;
- actionability;
- intervention success;
- control;
- causality;
- external generalization;
- formal topology;
- universal learning theory;
- universal cognition claims.

## Compact rule set

- observed ≠ robust;
- robust ≠ reusable;
- reusable ≠ registered;
- registered ≠ diagnostic-ready;
- diagnostic ≠ candidate-ready;
- candidate-ready ≠ repair-proven;
- repair hypothesis ≠ intervention success;
- intervention success ≠ causal generality;
- local empirical result ≠ universal theory.

## Canonical summary

> PAM/RIG claims should rise only when the evidence layer rises. The current program supports diagnostic reusable invariance within a declared artifact lineage; it does not yet support candidate-ready repair, actionability, causality, control, external generalization, or universal theory.
