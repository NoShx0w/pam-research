## Purpose

This note records the provenance and working interpretation of the current corpus ladder before the details become detached from the artifacts that produced them.

The shaped corpora are not neutral prompt variants. They are dialogue-derived affordance operators: model-proposed prompt preambles that condensed a prior phenomenological sketch of understanding, curiosity, play, invariance, metaphor, coherence, clarity, and self-awareness.

The baseline corpora are clean controls generated from the bare seed prompt:

```text
Explain how understanding emerges.
```

## Provenance Guardrail

The shaped preambles should not be treated as independently hand-authored experimental variables. They are interactional artifacts produced by a human phenomenological account and model-mediated linguistic distillation.

A careful provenance chain is:

```text
lived / phenomenological intuition
→ dialogue with model
→ conceptual stabilization
→ model-proposed preamble
→ generated corpus
→ recursive quench dynamics
→ observatory geometry / topology
```

The generated qualia visualization and related screenshots are provenance artifacts. They document the dialogue-derived origin of the corpus preambles. They are not runtime measurement inputs.

## Scientific Role of the Ladder

The corpus ladder should be read as an affordance experiment, not just a content experiment.

The question is not merely whether certain words appear more often. The question is whether preamble-conditioned symbolic affordances change:

- generator survival
- composition survival
- route-family transitions
- gateway accessibility
- canonical-family stability
- anchor persistence
- phase / seam structure

A compact framing:

```text
C0 asks what the model does without a shaped preamble.
C and Cp ask what explicit geometry + invariance + metaphor do.
Cp2 asks what happens when geometry is replaced by clarity.
```

## Current Interpretation of Cp2

Cp2 is scientifically important because early low-pressure runs produced undefined freeze / entropy coupling in some cases. This was first encountered as a technical failure, but the underlying behavior may be structural:

```text
NaN as absence of measurable coupling,
not absence of data.
```

Possible interpretation:

```text
Cp2’s clarity-stabilized invariance operator may smooth or suppress freeze transitions in low-pressure regimes.
```

This must remain provisional until the full campaign and comparisons are complete.
